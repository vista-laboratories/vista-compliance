💡 Compliance Tip of the Month

Plan for Antenna Changes Early!

A tiny tweak to an antenna can turn into a big compliance surprise. Even small shifts in gain, placement, or orientation can change RF exposure, detune your device, or trigger additional testing. Loop in your lab early if you’re considering updates — it’s much easier (and cheaper) to adjust the design before you’re deep into testing and certification.

and Ground Loops:

A sensor control unit was displaying conducted emissions  levels that didn’t line up with its typical profile.
