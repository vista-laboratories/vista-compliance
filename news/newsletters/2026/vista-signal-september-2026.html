💡 Compliance Tip of the Month

Control Lines & Clean Margins ⚡

When testing high-volume devices or communication control panels, compliance success often comes down to cable management. A stray cable can easily turn into an unintentional antenna, ruining your radiated-emissions margins before you even begin. To save precious chamber time, route auxiliary lines carefully, apply proper power-supply filtering early, and baseline your noise floor. A little prep upfront keeps your test run smooth and compliant.
